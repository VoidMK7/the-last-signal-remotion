# THE LAST SIGNAL
**Genre:** Science fiction / mystery / action / psychological thriller  
**Target duration:** Approximately 6 minutes 30 seconds  
**Setting:** Earth, 2189

## Premise
Humanity has built a seemingly flawless civilization governed by advanced artificial intelligence. One night, a research facility receives a signal from a scientist who disappeared 47 years earlier. His warning: “Whatever you do, don't open it.”

## Scene 1 — The world that never sleeps (00:00–00:45)
**Setting:** New Tokyo, midnight, 2189.

Open on black. A faint electronic pulse begins. A blue light appears in the distance; the camera pulls back to reveal a vast futuristic city. Flying vehicles pass between skyscrapers, holographic displays illuminate the streets, and the city operates with machine precision. A strange red flash crosses the sky unnoticed by everyone except one machine.

**Narrator:** “Humanity spent centuries trying to control its future. We conquered disease, eliminated scarcity, and taught machines to think. We believed there was nothing left to discover.”

## Scene 2 — The impossible transmission (00:45–01:30)
**Setting:** International Deep Space Research Facility.

Dr. Elias Voss, 52, works alone. A monitor detects a signal from an impossible location. A voice emerges through static.

**Unknown voice:** “Elias... if you're hearing this, then the experiment worked.”

Elias recognizes his former mentor, Professor Adrian Kane, missing for 47 years.

**Unknown voice:** “Listen carefully. We thought we were opening a gateway. We were wrong. Something was already waiting on the other side.”

The screen cuts to black. Text: **DO NOT OPEN THE GATE.**

## Scene 3 — The forgotten experiment (01:30–02:15)
Elias opens an encrypted archive. A recording reveals Project Horizon: a 30-metre circular machine built to transmit information through a controlled space-time distortion. During its first test, the machine detected another dimension. The recording glitches; a shadow appears in the gateway and the research team vanishes. In the final frame, Adrian looks directly into the camera.

**Elias:** “That's impossible...”

## Scene 4 — The awakening (02:15–03:00)
Elias enters the abandoned Project Horizon facility. Dust, flickering lights, damaged cables, and mysterious symbols surround the dormant circular machine. He inserts the recovered signal. Blue energy travels around the ring, followed by red. The chamber vibrates.

**System:** “PROJECT HORIZON ONLINE.”

A countdown begins. The machine is opening itself.

## Scene 5 — Something is looking back (03:00–04:00)
The portal opens onto a landscape where stars move in the wrong direction, mountains float upside down, and rivers flow into the sky. A vast silhouette appears.

**Unknown entity:** “You took your time.”

**Elias:** “Who are you?”

**Unknown entity:** “We are what your world forgot.”

The portal expands. The system reports external control.

## Scene 6 — The truth about Adrian (04:00–04:45)
A human figure appears inside the gateway: Adrian, unchanged after 47 years.

**Elias:** “Adrian?”

**Adrian:** “Elias. You shouldn't have come.”

**Elias:** “What happened to you?”

**Adrian:** “We weren't the first ones to discover this place. We were the ones who were discovered.”

Adrian explains that the gateway connects realities, not locations. The signal has helped the entity locate Earth.

**Adrian:** “Close it. Now.”

## Scene 7 — The final decision (04:45–05:45)
The entity begins crossing into the laboratory. The system announces containment failure in 30 seconds. Elias reaches the emergency terminal. Manual shutdown will destroy the facility and may trap Adrian.

**Adrian:** “Some doors should remain closed.”

Elias confirms the shutdown. The chamber turns white; the portal collapses in a shockwave.

## Scene 8 — The last signal (05:45–06:30)
Days later, news reports describe an underground energy accident. Elias is declared missing. Life continues. The camera pulls away from Earth, past the Moon and Mars, into an unexplored region. A red light appears, then thousands. A colossal structure with hundreds of gateways emerges from the darkness.

**Unknown voice:** “You closed the first door.”

A pause.

**Unknown voice:** “But you forgot... there are others.”

Cut to black.

**TITLE: THE LAST SIGNAL**  
**END... OR THE BEGINNING?**
