# Audio cue sheet

The project includes `public/audio/original-score.mp3`, a 60-second original synthesized ambient music bed looped under the film. It is a placeholder score, not recorded instruments or voice acting.

| Moment | Music / effects direction |
|---|---|
| Family home | Felt piano, low warm pad, distant traffic, kettle, fan, footsteps. |
| Breakup | Café room tone and traffic; pull ambience down after Nia leaves; leave a quiet piano note. |
| Learning montage | Restrained percussion built from keyboard taps, tools, bus indicators and page turns. |
| Market setbacks | Muted notification texture and rain; cut to silence when Malik stops chasing losses. Avoid casino sounds. |
| Months passing | Rain, morning birds, workshop ambience and gradually warmer strings. |
| $100M reveal | Restrained orchestral swell, then near-silence and one piano note. |
| Family reunion | Warm strings, soft wordless choir pad, workshop door, tools and family laughter. |
| Finale | Resolved piano theme, sunset city ambience and two seconds of silence. |

Add recorded dialogue/narration and dedicated sound effects before release. Use original or properly licensed audio only.
